02.00 · TOOLS

What it does

Battle maps

Grid combat with tokens, fog of war, vision and areas, for in-person or play-by-post games.

World map

Paint a hex map, mark towns and dungeons, link each place to its battle map, and reveal it to players as they explore.

Share it

Post an image to Discord or send a link. Players see only what you choose to show.
